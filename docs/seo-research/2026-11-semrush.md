Pulled during the Semrush trial so the plan does not depend on it later.

## Key findings

- Semrush shows no top 100 rankings yet for adsncodes.com (site is new; Search Console shows early impressions and clicks).

- hellosocial.ae ranks 1 to 2 for the biggest social media terms with a weak link profile (mostly UAE directories). Strong on page focus plus local directories is enough to compete on social media terms.
