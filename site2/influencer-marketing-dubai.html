Overview

Reach is easy to buy. Trust is the part that sells.

In the UAE, people check what a creator they follow thinks before they book a clinic, try a restaurant or buy a product. The right creator gives your brand that borrowed trust. The wrong one gives you views from the wrong country and nothing else.

We look past follower counts at where each audience actually lives, the language they speak and how they engage. Then we brief creators properly, keep their voice, make sure every post meets UAE Media Council rules, and turn the best content into ads so it keeps working after the post goes live.

What's included

Influencer marketing services

  • Creator sourcing and vetting

    Audience location, engagement quality and brand fit checked before anyone is booked, from nano to well known names.

  • Campaign strategy

    Goals, creator mix, content angles and timing around launches, Ramadan, Eid, DSF and National Day.

  • Briefing and content approval

    Clear briefs that keep the creator's own style, with your approval before anything is posted.

  • Advertiser Permit compliance

    We work with creators who hold a valid UAE Media Council Advertiser Permit and make sure every post is disclosed correctly.

  • Arabic and English creators

    Emirati, Arab and expat creators matched to who you actually sell to.

  • Paid amplification

    Top creator posts run as Spark Ads and partnership ads, so the best content reaches more of the right people.

  • Event and launch activations

    Creator visits, store openings and launch events with same day content.

  • Tracking and reporting

    Codes, links and pixels per creator, so you see the clicks, leads and sales each creator brought in.
