Overview

Online, the photo is the product.

Shoppers cannot pick your product up, so the images do the job a shop assistant would: show the size, the finish, the label and how it looks in use. Weak photos raise returns and lower conversion, and on Amazon and Noon they push you below sellers with better images.

We shoot to each marketplace's image rules, light reflective and glass products properly, and retouch every file for dust, colour and labels. Then we add the images that actually persuade: lifestyle scenes, scale shots, details and short product videos for ads.
