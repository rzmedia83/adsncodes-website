The best SEO agencies in Dubai: a 2026 shortlist

The right SEO agency depends on your size, market and how fast you need results. Here are five worth a call, and who each one suits.

By Razal Mohammed · 5 November 2026 · 7 min read

Search for the best SEO company in Dubai and you will find lists of twenty or thirty names, most of them written by the agencies on the list. This shortlist is smaller on purpose: five agencies with a clear profile, grouped by the kind of business each one suits best.

Full disclosure: we are one of them, and we have put ourselves first. We have tried to describe everyone fairly, using what each agency and the regional trade press say about them. Facts on other agencies come from the sources listed at the end, so check anything that matters to you directly with them.

1. Ads n' Codes

Best for small and growing UAE businesses that want local SEO, Google Ads and a website that converts, handled by one team.

  • Based at B Hive, BurJuman Business Tower in Dubai, founded in 2024.
  • Focuses on local SEO and Google Business Profile for businesses that sell in specific emirates and areas, including Abu Dhabi.
  • Builds area and service pages in English and Arabic, fixes technical issues and runs Google Ads alongside SEO, so you get enquiries while rankings build.
  • Reports calls, WhatsApp chats and enquiries from search, not just rankings.
  • A newer and smaller team than the others on this list, which suits businesses that want direct contact with the person doing the work.

2. SEO Sherpa

Best for large brands and enterprises that want an award winning, SEO only specialist.

  • Helping clients since 2012, with offices in Dubai, London, Miami, Riyadh and Singapore, according to the agency.
  • Won Best Large SEO Agency at the MENA Search Awards 2025, along with campaign awards in health, real estate, Arabic content and digital PR.
  • Lists clients including Amazon, HSBC, Nissan and Farfetch.
  • A strong fit if SEO is a major channel for your business and you have the budget for a large specialist team.

3. Chain Reaction

Best for regional and multinational brands that need Arabic SEO and search across several MENA markets.

  • Founded by Saif Jarad, named as founder and CEO by Campaign Middle East.
  • Named Best Large SEO Agency in the MENA region at the MENA Search Awards 2019, one of five awards that year, according to Campaign Middle East.
  • Award winning work includes Arabic SEO for Samsung Levant and search campaigns for KFC.
  • Worth a call if you need SEO in Arabic and English across the Gulf and Levant.

4. Prism Digital

Best for hospitality, retail and e-commerce brands that want SEO as part of a full digital agency.

  • Based at the World Trade Center, Sheikh Zayed Road, Dubai.
  • Offers technical, local, e-commerce and Arabic SEO, plus AI search optimisation, content, link building and digital PR.
  • Publishes case studies for UAE brands such as Exotica and Mamlakat Al Oud, and shows hospitality clients including Hilton and Jumeirah.
  • Also runs social media, paid ads and web development, which helps if you want one agency for everything.

5. Traffic Digital

Best for real estate and larger businesses that want SEO inside a performance marketing plan.

  • Founded in 2009 and headquartered in JLT, Dubai, with offices in Riyadh, Jeddah and Karachi, according to its directory listing.
  • Known mainly for performance marketing, SEO and paid media.
  • Lists real estate developers and brokerages among its clients.

How to choose an SEO agency in Dubai

  • Ask what they will do in the first 90 days, in writing. Good agencies are specific: which pages, which fixes, which searches.
  • Ask how they report. Rankings alone are not the goal. Calls, enquiries and sales from search are.
  • Be wary of guaranteed first positions. Nobody controls Google's rankings.
  • Check whether they do Arabic SEO properly, with Arabic keyword research, not translation, if your customers search in Arabic.
  • Ask who will work on your account every week, and speak to that person before you sign.
  • Make sure your Google Business Profile, Search Console and analytics stay in your name.

Questions

It depends on your size and goals. Large brands with big budgets often choose award winning specialists such as SEO Sherpa or Chain Reaction. Smaller businesses that sell locally usually get more attention and faster local results from a smaller team that handles SEO, Google Ads and the website together.

Local map results often move within one to three months. Competitive searches usually take four to six months or more, depending on your website, your competitors and how much content is built.

Google Ads brings enquiries within days while you pay. SEO takes longer but keeps bringing visits without paying per click. Most growing businesses run both, using ads while SEO builds up.

If a meaningful share of your customers search in Arabic, yes. Arabic searches have their own keywords and competitors, and are often less competitive than English ones.
