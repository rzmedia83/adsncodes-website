5x online sales for a natural children's food brand

A founder with a genuinely good product and online sales that weren't reflecting it. Positioning and content changed that.

Food and e-commerce · Client name kept private

Results

The numbers

  • 5xonline sales within a few months
  • 100%natural food for kids, the product at the heart of the story
  • Organicgrowth through content and online channels alone

Where they were

The founder had built a genuinely good product: 100% natural food for children. But online sales did not reflect the quality of what was in the pack, and the founder came to us for professional help to grow.

What we did

We started by understanding the brand end to end: the founder's story, what parents worry about when they choose food for their children, and why they buy. Parents buying for their kids read labels closely and trust brands that are open about what goes in.

From there we rebuilt the positioning and planned the content around it, from what to say to how often to say it, so every post worked toward the same clear message.

  • Positioning built on what parents care about, not on product features alone.
  • A content plan that told the founder's story and answered parents' real questions.
  • A consistent posting rhythm across the brand's online channels.

What changed

Online sales grew five times within a few months, through content and online channels alone.

What other founder led brands can take from this

  • A good product is not enough on its own. People need to understand why it matters to them.
  • The founder's story is often the brand's strongest asset, especially in food and family products.
  • Consistency beats volume. A clear message repeated well does more than lots of unrelated posts.
