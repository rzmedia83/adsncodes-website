WhatsApp Business Solution Provider in the UAE: what they do and how to choose one

To use the WhatsApp Business Platform, most businesses go through a Business Solution Provider. The choice decides your monthly cost, what your team can do and how hard it is to leave. Here is how to choose well.

By Razal Mohammed · 8 November 2026 · 6 min read

Once a business outgrows the WhatsApp Business app, the next step is the WhatsApp Business Platform. Meta does not give you an app for it. You reach it through software: a shared inbox, a CRM, a marketing tool or an AI agent. The companies Meta approves to connect businesses this way are called Business Solution Providers, or BSPs.

What a WhatsApp Business Solution Provider does

  • Connects your number to the WhatsApp Business Platform and helps with Meta business verification.
  • Gives your team an inbox to answer chats, assign them and see history.
  • Lets you create and submit message templates for approval, such as booking reminders or order updates.
  • Sends broadcasts and campaigns to customers who have agreed to hear from you.
  • Connects WhatsApp to your CRM, booking system, store or ads, and sometimes adds chatbots or AI.

Do you need a BSP, or can you go direct?

Meta also offers the Cloud API directly to businesses. Going direct avoids a provider's markup, but there is no inbox or interface, so you need developers to build or connect one. For most UAE small and mid sized businesses, a provider or a ready made agent is simpler. For businesses with a development team, direct access gives the most control.

Questions to ask before you choose

  • What exactly will I pay? Ask for the software fee, any markup on Meta's message charges, setup fees and the cost per extra user, in writing.
  • Who owns the number and the WhatsApp Business account? It should be registered to your company in your own Meta Business Manager, so you can move providers later.
  • Can we leave easily? Ask how a number is migrated out and whether chat history can be exported.
  • Does it handle Arabic properly? Right to left templates, Arabic labels and agents that can read Arabic messages.
  • What integrations come ready? Your CRM, Shopify, booking tool, Google Sheets and Meta ads click to WhatsApp tracking.
  • Is there local support? Someone who answers in your working hours when a template is rejected or a number is flagged.
  • Can it automate real conversations? Many providers offer menu bots. Fewer can run an AI agent that answers from your own prices and policies.

Where an AI agent fits

A provider gives you the pipe and the inbox. What fills most of the inbox is the same set of questions: prices, timings, availability, location, delivery areas. An AI agent on the platform answers those in seconds, in Arabic and English, books or qualifies the lead and passes the conversation to your team only when it needs a person. When we build a WhatsApp AI agent for a client, we set it up on Meta's official platform under the client's own business account, so the number, the data and the relationship with Meta stay theirs. If you already use a provider, we can usually work alongside it.
