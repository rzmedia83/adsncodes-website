Overview

In aesthetics, the clinic that replies first usually books the consultation.

Aesthetic clients rarely enquire at one clinic. They see an ad or a reel, message three or four places about the same treatment and book with whoever answers clearly and quickly. Most of those messages arrive in the evening, long after your coordinators have gone home.

The aesthetic agent replies the moment the message lands, in the client's language. It explains your treatments in your clinic's tone, answers the questions clients ask before they commit, and books a consultation or session from your live calendar. Clients on a course of treatments are reminded when their next session is due, so fewer of them drift away halfway through.

What's included

What the aesthetic clinic agent does

  • Instant reply to every ad lead

    Click to WhatsApp ads and Instagram enquiries get a helpful reply in seconds, day or night, so your ad spend is not wasted on slow replies.

  • Treatments explained in your tone

    Skin, laser, body and injectable treatments as cards, with your prices or packages and what to expect on the day.

  • Consultations booked by practitioner

    Clients choose a doctor or therapist, including a female practitioner where they prefer, from live availability.

  • Course and package follow up

    Reminders when the next laser or skin session is due, using Meta approved templates, so clients finish what they started.

  • Careful with claims

    No promised results and no client photos in the chat. Wording stays within what your licensing authority allows for medical advertising.

  • Handover to your coordinators

    Price negotiations, medical history questions and VIP clients go to your team in the same chat.
