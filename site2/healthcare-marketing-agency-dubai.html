Overview

Patients choose the doctor they already trust.

Before booking, patients in the UAE watch the doctor on Instagram, read Google reviews and message the clinic on WhatsApp to ask about prices and insurance. Clinics that answer clearly and quickly win those bookings.

We film doctors explaining treatments in plain words, run patient campaigns on Meta and Google, grow reviews and local search, and set up a WhatsApp agent that shares prices, introduces doctors and books appointments. Health advertising in the UAE is regulated, so claims and approvals are planned into every campaign.
