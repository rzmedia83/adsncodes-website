Overview

Physio clinics lose patients between sessions, not at the first booking.

A typical treatment plan is six to ten visits. Patients cancel when work runs late, forget the next appointment or stop once the pain eases, and every gap in the plan is an empty slot for your therapist and a slower recovery for the patient.

The physio agent books the first assessment the moment someone messages, then keeps the plan on track: it suggests the next session after each visit, sends reminders, and lets patients move an appointment in the chat instead of simply not showing up. Your therapists see a fuller diary and your reception spends less time on the phone.

What's included

What the physiotherapy clinic agent does

  • Assessments booked in seconds

    New patients choose the reason for the visit, a therapist or the first free slot, at the clinic or for a home visit.

  • Follow up sessions kept on track

    After each visit the next session is offered, and reminders go out before every appointment.

  • Easy rescheduling

    Patients move an appointment in the chat, so the slot is freed for someone else instead of lost to a no show.

  • Therapist preference respected

    Patients can ask for a male or female therapist, or the one they saw last time.

  • Insurance and referral questions

    Which insurers you work with and whether a doctor's referral is needed, from the list your team keeps.

  • No clinical advice

    Pain, injuries and exercises are always passed to your therapists, never answered by the agent.
