Overview

Paid search reaches people who are already looking for you.

Someone typing "emergency dentist Dubai Marina" or "car service Al Quoz" wants to buy today. PPC puts you at the top of that search within days, while SEO takes months to get there. The catch is that Google Ads spends money quickly when it is set up loosely: broad keywords, no negative keywords and no conversion tracking.

We set up tracking first, so calls, WhatsApp chats and form sends are counted as results. Then we build tight campaigns by service and by area, in English and Arabic, and check search terms every week to cut the clicks that never turn into customers. You own the Google Ads account and see every dirham spent.
