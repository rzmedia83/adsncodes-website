Marketing andtechnology,one roof

Most businesses buy their marketing from one company and their website and systems from another, then lose customers in the gap between them. We started Ads n' Codes to close it.

The name says what we do.

  • Ads

    The work that gets people to notice you: social media, paid campaigns, video, branding and content.

  • Codes

    The work that gets them to choose you: websites, search, AI and the systems that reply and follow up.

  • The asterisk

    Look at the o in Codes. In writing, an asterisk points to the detail behind a headline. For us that detail is the technology: the ad makes a promise, and the system behind it keeps it.

Creative instinct. Engineering discipline.

Ads n' Codes was founded in 2024 by Razal Mohammed, an engineer who spent years in film and content production before building a marketing and technology agency.

That mix runs through the work. The creative agency side decides what makes people stop and care. Engineering thinking makes sure the website, the ads and the systems behind them keep working when it matters.

Strategists, designers, photographers, editors, developers and ad specialists work together on every account, with one point of contact owning it from the first brief to the monthly report.
