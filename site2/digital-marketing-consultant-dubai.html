Overview

Most businesses do not need more marketing. They need the right marketing.

Many UAE businesses run ads, post on social media and pay for a website, yet still cannot say which of it brings customers. Agencies sell the service they offer, freelancers do the task they are given, and nobody looks at the whole picture.

A consultant starts there. Razal Mohammed, our founder, reviews your channels, your numbers and your competitors, then gives you a written plan for the next 90 days: what to keep, what to stop, what to build and how to measure it. You can carry it out with your own team, or have Ads n' Codes do it with you. Either way, the advice comes first and is not shaped by what we want to sell.

What's included

How a consultant engagement works

  • Marketing audit

    A full review of your website, Google presence, social media, ads, tracking and how enquiries are handled.

  • 90 day plan

    A written plan with priorities, budgets by channel and the numbers to watch, in plain language.

  • Channel decisions

    Honest answers on Google or Meta, SEO or ads, agency or in house, and which platforms to drop.

  • Tracking and reporting

    Analytics, Pixel and call and WhatsApp tracking set up so every decision is based on real enquiries.

  • Monthly advisory

    A monthly review call and report, acting as the senior marketing lead you have not hired yet.

  • Hands on delivery

    When you want it done for you, our team runs the ads, content, website and automation from the same plan.

Process

How we work

  1. Call

    A short call about your business, goals and what you have tried so far.

  2. Audit

    We review your channels, data and competitors and note what is working and what is wasting money.

  3. Plan

    You receive a written 90 day plan and we walk you through it.

  4. Carry it out

    Your team, our team or both put the plan into action, with a monthly review.
