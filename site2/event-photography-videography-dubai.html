Overview

An event lasts a day. The content should last months.

Most event content gets posted once and forgotten. We plan coverage around what you'll use afterwards: speaker clips for LinkedIn, reels for Instagram, a highlight film for next year's sponsors and photos your PR team can actually send out.

We also handle the side most crews don't: event branding, the promotion campaign that sells tickets or fills seats, registration pages and WhatsApp RSVPs, so one team runs the whole thing.
