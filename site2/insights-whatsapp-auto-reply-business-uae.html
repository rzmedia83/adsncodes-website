WhatsApp auto reply for business: how to set it up, and when it stops being enough

Most UAE customers message before they call. An auto reply tells them you got the message. Here is how to switch it on in five minutes, and the point where it starts costing you leads.

By Razal Mohammed · 8 November 2026 · 6 min read

If your business runs on WhatsApp, the first thing a customer sees after messaging you matters. A clinic in Al Barsha, a salon in JLT or a restaurant in Al Nahda all get the same questions at all hours: are you open, how much is it, can I book for tonight. An automatic reply means nobody is left looking at two grey ticks.

The free WhatsApp Business app has three tools for this. The regular personal WhatsApp app has none of them, so if you still run the business from a personal number, moving to WhatsApp Business is step one.

1. Away message: the out of hours auto reply

The away message sends automatically when someone messages you while you are closed. Open the WhatsApp Business app and go to Tools, then Away message, and turn on Send away message.

  • Write a short message that says when you will reply, and what to do if it is urgent.
  • Under Schedule, choose Outside of business hours. This uses the hours saved in your business profile, so set those first.
  • Under Recipients, leave it on Everyone unless you have a good reason to exclude someone.

Example: "Thanks for messaging. We are closed right now and will reply from 9am. For urgent bookings, call +971 XX XXX XXXX."

2. Greeting message: the first hello

The greeting message goes to anyone who messages you for the first time, or after 14 days without a chat. Go to Tools, then Greeting message, and turn on Send greeting message. Keep it to two lines: who you are and what they can ask you.

3. Quick replies: saved answers your team sends

Quick replies are saved messages your team sends by typing a forward slash and a shortcut, such as /location or /timings. Go to Tools, then Quick replies, and add your ten most common answers. They do not send on their own, but they cut reply time for whoever is on the phone.

What the built in auto reply cannot do

  • It sends the same fixed text to everyone. It cannot answer the actual question the customer asked.
  • It cannot take a booking, check a slot, share a price for a specific service or confirm an order.
  • It only works while the phone running the app is online. Battery savers that close the app can stop it.
  • During opening hours it stays silent, which is exactly when your team is busiest and replies slow down.
  • It cannot pass a hot lead to the right person or record it anywhere.

So an auto reply stops the customer feeling ignored. It does not stop them booking with the competitor who answered their question first.

When a business outgrows auto reply

  • You get more WhatsApp messages than your team can answer within a few minutes.
  • Most messages are the same ten questions: prices, location, timings, availability, delivery areas.
  • You run Meta or Google ads that send people to WhatsApp, so every slow reply is paid traffic going cold.
  • Enquiries arrive at night and on weekends, and by morning the customer has gone elsewhere.

At that point the next step is an assistant that actually replies: it reads the question, answers from your real prices and timings, shows options, takes the booking and hands over to a person when needed. That is what we build as a WhatsApp AI agent. It runs on your own number through Meta's official WhatsApp Business Platform.
