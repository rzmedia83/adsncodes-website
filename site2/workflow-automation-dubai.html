Overview

Your team should sell and serve, not copy and paste.

In most UAE businesses the same tasks repeat all day: copying leads from WhatsApp and forms into a sheet or CRM, chasing payments, sending quotes, reminding customers and putting together the weekly report. Each one is small. Together they eat hours and cause missed follow ups.

We map how work moves through your business, then automate the steps that follow clear rules, using tools such as n8n, Make, Zapier and Microsoft Power Automate, connected to the apps you already use. Where a step needs judgement, AI handles the reading and sorting, and a person approves what matters.

What's included

Workflow automation services

  • Lead routing and CRM updates

    Every enquiry from WhatsApp, forms, calls and ads lands in your CRM with the right owner and a follow up task.

  • Quotes, invoices and payment reminders

    Quotations and invoices generated from your data, sent on time, with polite reminders until paid.

  • Booking and order workflows

    Confirmations, reminders, reschedules and kitchen or dispatch updates without manual messages.

  • Reports and dashboards

    Sales, leads and ad results pulled together automatically and sent every morning or week.

  • Documents and approvals

    Contracts, forms and approvals that move to the right person and get filed automatically.

  • AI assisted workflows

    AI reads emails, PDFs and messages, extracts the details and fills your systems, with human checks.
