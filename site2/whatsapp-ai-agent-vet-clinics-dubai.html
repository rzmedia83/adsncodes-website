Overview

Pet owners book with the vet that answers first, and stay for years.

Vet clinics in the UAE handle a mix that few other clinics do: consultations, vaccinations, travel paperwork, grooming, boarding and the occasional emergency, all arriving in the same WhatsApp inbox. Routine requests pile up while your team is busy with the animals already in the clinic.

The vet agent takes care of the routine part. It asks for the pet's name, species, breed and age once, books the right service with the right vet or groomer, and reminds the owner when the next vaccine or groom is due. Anything that sounds urgent is never put in a booking flow: the owner gets your emergency number and your team is alerted at once.

What's included

What the vet clinic agent does

  • Every service in one chat

    Consultations, vaccinations, travel certificates, dental care, grooming and boarding, each with its own short booking flow.

  • Pet details kept on file

    Name, species, breed and age are asked once, so returning owners book in a few taps.

  • Vaccine and grooming reminders

    A reminder when the next vaccine, booster or groom is due, using Meta approved templates.

  • Urgent cases sent to people

    Symptoms that sound urgent get your emergency number straight away and an alert to your team.

  • Boarding requests handled

    Dates, pet details and vaccination status collected up front, then confirmed by your team.

  • Synced with your clinic software

    Bookings land in your vet practice system through its API, so nothing is typed twice.
