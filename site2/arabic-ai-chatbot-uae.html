An AI assistant that replies in Arabic and English, understands how people in the Gulf actually write, and answers from your real services, prices and availability, on WhatsApp and your website.

Get a proposalGet a free audit
Overview

Half your customers would rather write in Arabic.

Many UAE customers switch between Arabic and English in the same message, write Arabic in Latin letters, or use Gulf words a translation tool misreads. Old menu bots and English only chat widgets lose them, and a human team cannot reply in both languages at midnight.

We build AI assistants that read Gulf Arabic, Modern Standard Arabic, Arabizi and English, reply in the language the customer used, and answer from your own content: services, prices, timings, locations and policies. They take bookings, qualify leads and pass the conversation to a person when it needs one, with the full chat history.

What's included

What the Arabic AI assistant does

  • Arabic and English replies

    Answers in the customer's own language, including Gulf dialect and Arabizi, and switches when they do.

  • Trained on your business

    Answers come from your services, prices, menus, listings and policies, not from guesses.

  • WhatsApp and website

    Runs on your WhatsApp number through Meta's official platform, and as a chat on your website.

  • Bookings and lead capture

    Checks availability, books appointments, takes orders and records every lead in your CRM or sheet.

  • Handover to your team

    Passes the chat to the right person with a summary when a customer asks for a human or the case needs one.

  • Open around the clock

    Replies in seconds at night, on weekends and during Ramadan hours, when your team is offline.
