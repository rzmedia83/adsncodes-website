AI that knowsyour business

AI agents and WhatsApp systems for any business that lives on enquiries: real estate, clinics, restaurants, retail, automotive, education and more. Built around your services, prices and the way you speak to customers.

Everyone runs ads. Very few answer the leads in time.

Someone sees your ad at 11 pm, sends a message, and waits. By the time your team replies the next morning, they've booked somewhere else.

Our systems reply straight away, in a way that sounds like your business. They answer questions, check what's available, take the booking or order, and save everything where your team will see it. When a customer needs a person, the chat moves to your staff with the details already there.

What we build

Four kinds of systems

  • AI agents

    Trained on your services, prices, policies and tone. They answer questions on WhatsApp and your website the way a well briefed team member would.

    For example: a clinic agent that explains treatments, prices and insurance, then books the visit.

  • WhatsApp automation

    Complete flows that run inside WhatsApp on your existing business number, from the first message to a confirmed booking or order.

    For example: restaurant orders that land directly on the kitchen screen.

  • Workflow automation

    The repetitive follow up work your team does every day, done on time without anyone remembering to do it.

    For example: reminders the day before an appointment, and a nudge to leads who went quiet.

  • Integrations

    Every lead and conversation goes where your team already works, so nothing is typed in twice.

    For example: Zoho, HubSpot, clinic booking software, restaurant POS, Google Sheets.

Where it all lands

Your team only does the part that needs a person

The agent doesn't just chat. It finishes the job inside the software you already run, so nothing is copied, typed in twice or forgotten.

  • Real estate

    Qualified leads reach the right agent

    Budget, area, finance and timeline arrive with the shortlist and a booked viewing, routed by area and language and written into your CRM.

  • Clinics

    Bookings land in your clinic system or CRM

    Every appointment and enquiry is saved in your existing booking software, with the patient's details and the full conversation.

  • Restaurants

    Orders go straight to your POS and kitchen

    The order arrives priced, with the address and payment details, in the system your staff already use. They just prepare and deliver.

  • Always in sync

    Menus, slots and listings update themselves

    Dishes and prices, doctors and time slots, properties, products and stock sync automatically from your systems. Change it once and the agent already knows.

  • Built your way

    Customised to your business and your customers

    Every flow is designed around how your customers actually ask and buy: your questions, offers, upsells, rules, handover times and the systems you already use. After launch we study real conversations and keep refining it.

  • Multi branch

    One WhatsApp number, every branch

    Customers share their location and the agent finds the nearest branch, shows what that branch has available and books or orders there.

  • Any language

    Replies in the customer's language

    Arabic, English, Hindi and more. Customers write the way they normally would and get a natural reply.

  • Reminders

    Reminders and reviews, sent for you

    Appointment and order reminders cut no shows, and a review request goes out after the visit or delivery, using Meta approved templates.

  • Repeat customers

    Regulars reorder in one tap

    Names, addresses and past orders are remembered, so returning customers skip the questions and book or reorder in seconds.

  • Privacy first

    Sensitive details stay out of the chat

    Emirates ID, insurance and payment card numbers are never asked for in WhatsApp. Live data stays in your own systems.

  • Official

    Built on Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform

    Your own business number, verified and compliant with WhatsApp policies, so nothing gets blocked after launch.
