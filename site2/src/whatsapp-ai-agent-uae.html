The problem nobody talks about

Your WhatsApp never stops. You can't answer all of it.

Enquiries come in from everywhere: people who found you on Google Maps, Instagram and Facebook messages, website visitors, friends of happy customers, regulars asking the same questions again, and ads if you run them. They arrive at 10 pm, on Fridays, during lunch, while you are with a client or on site. The owner is busy running the business, the team is busy serving the customers already in front of them, and by the time someone replies, the new customer has messaged three competitors and gone with whoever answered first.

A slow reply doesn't just lose one sale. Every customer who gave up waiting was already interested, and you earned that interest with years of work, good reviews and word of mouth. If you also run ads, it quietly doubles what each customer costs you.

Work out what slow replies cost you

Research published in Harvard Business Review, based on more than a million inbound leads, found that companies contacting a lead within an hour were about seven times more likely to qualify it than those who waited even an hour longer, and over sixty times more likely than those who waited a day.

  • 0 to 5 min

  • 1 hour

  • Next day

    The customer has moved on. Most of the chance to win them is gone.
