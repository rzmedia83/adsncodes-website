How it works

How we estimate it

People who message a business usually message two or three at once, and most go with whoever answers first. Research published in Harvard Business Review, based on more than a million inbound leads, found that companies replying within an hour were about seven times more likely to qualify a lead than those who waited longer, and over sixty times more likely than those who waited a day.

We use much more cautious numbers than that. The calculator assumes an enquiry answered within five minutes is as likely as possible to become a customer, and gives slower replies a lower chance:

100%within 5 minutes
85%5 to 30 minutes
70%30 to 60 minutes
55%1 to 3 hours
45%later the same day
35%next day

Enquiries outside working hours count as next day replies unless you tick that you already cover nights and weekends. To keep it realistic, the estimate never assumes more than double your current customers. It is an estimate to help you decide, not a promise of results.
