Overview

Dubai decides where to eat on Instagram and TikTok.

Most diners in the UAE find a new place through a reel, a friend's story or a food creator. Then they check Google Maps and order on WhatsApp or a delivery app.

We shoot food and the room, plan social around your menu and the UAE calendar from Ramadan to the Dubai Food Festival, bring in creators who match your crowd, and set up WhatsApp ordering that sends orders straight to the kitchen, without delivery app commissions.

After the click

Every order taken, even in the rush.

Your ads and Instagram bring hungry customers. Our WhatsApp ordering agent takes the order in seconds: menu cards, typed orders understood, delivery or pickup, and the order sent straight to your kitchen, without aggregator commission. Try it as a customer.
