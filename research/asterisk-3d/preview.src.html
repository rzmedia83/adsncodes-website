Speed and SEO

About 3 KB of script, no library, no images. It loads after the page and draws only while it is on screen, so text, headings and links that Google reads stay exactly as they are.

Phones

It runs on phones too, at a lighter resolution to save battery. If a phone cannot draw 3D, or the visitor has asked for less motion, it shows a still asterisk instead.
